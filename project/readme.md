# The Forgotten Citadel - Text RPG

## Game Idea & Narrative
"The Forgotten Citadel" is a text-based fantasy RPG built in Python. The player controls Arthur, a young adventurer exploring an ancient castle to retrieve the legendary *Crystal of Fates* to cure illnesses and bring balance back to the realm.

## Sustainable Development Goal (SDG)
This game supports **SDG 3: Good Health and Well-being**. 
The main objective revolves around finding remedies (Health Potion) and the Crystal of Fates to restore health and prevent disease. The narrative highlights the importance of resourcefulness, resilience, and medical discovery in fantasy form.

## Objectives & Functionalities
**Objective:** Navigate through Citadel rooms, collect key items (Golden Key, Iron Sword, Health Potion), and unlock the final exit.
**Multiple Paths:** Players can choose different navigation orders between rooms to solve the citadel's puzzle.
**Save/Load System:** Save progress to `savegame.json` and resume anytime.
**Text File Integration:** Reads story intro and instructions dynamically from `intro.txt` and `instructions.txt`.

## How to Run
Ensure Python 3.x is installed.
Run `python main.py` in your terminal.
Follow the on-screen menu prompts.
